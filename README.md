
# Starfall — A 3D Space Sandbox

## Play Now

**Live Demo:** [https://cookierider282.github.io/starfall/](https://cookierider282.github.io/starfall/)

---

**Starfall** is a procedural 3D space sandbox I built from scratch in JavaScript. Fly around an infinite galaxy, build ships, fight AI civilizations, terraform planets, and manipulate entire star systems. Nothing is scripted. Everything runs on its own.

## What You Can Do

### Explore
- Procedurally generated star systems with planets, moons, asteroid belts, nebulae, and black holes.
- Random cosmic events and anomalies.
- No two playthroughs look the same.

### Fly
- Full 3D flight with gravity and orbital mechanics.
- Atmospheric entry, planetary landing, and docking.
- Advanced combat maneuvering.

### Build Ships
- Modular ship construction using individual parts.
- Engines, fuel tanks, reactors, weapons, and shields.
- Real-time 3D previews of your builds.
- Ship performance changes based on the components you install.

### Watch Civilizations Do Their Thing
- AI civilizations operate independently with their own governments, economies, and militaries.
- Factions expand, collapse, declare war, and form alliances.
- Interact with civilizations or watch the galaxy evolve on its own.

### Engineer Planets
- Terraform planetary atmospheres.
- Move moons between orbits.
- Build artificial planetary rings.
- Detonate stars (carefully).
- Construct Dyson swarms for passive income.

### Build Bases
- Establish planetary surface bases and orbital stations.
- Refuel, craft, trade, and upgrade equipment.
- Use the beacon system to navigate back to important locations.

### Trade and Mine
- Gather resources through mining, salvaging, and crafting.
- Explore markets with prices that vary by planet.
- Take advantage of dynamically shifting prices.
- Progress through a long-term technology and upgrade tree.

### Deploy Drones
- Deploy combat and harvesting drones.
- Command AI wingmen that fight alongside you.
- Participate in large-scale faction fleet battles.

### Interface and Systems
- HUD with an integrated minimap.
- First-person cockpit view.
- Mission system.
- Achievement system.
- Full save/load support for the entire world state.

## The Point

There is no scripted story. The galaxy runs on layers of interconnected systems, and whatever emerges from those interactions becomes your experience.

Civilizations evolve, economies shift, wars break out, and star systems change. You are free to explore, build, experiment, or simply observe.

**No two players will have the same experience.**

## Tech Stack

- **HTML5**
- **JavaScript (ES Modules)**
- **WebGL** — 3D rendering
- **Custom-built systems** — Procedural generation, physics, AI, and simulation
